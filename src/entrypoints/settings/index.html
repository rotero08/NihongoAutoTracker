Video Tracking

Manual mode
The timer in the player counts real watch-time (skips excluded). When a video ends — or you click the Log button — the watched minutes enter the queue. Nothing is sent until you click Send here or in the popup.
Auto mode
When your playback position passes the threshold (e.g. 95%), the extension immediately logs the video's full duration to NihongoTracker — no review step. Works only on videos detected as Japanese.
